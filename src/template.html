About this list

Trial Watch lists head and neck and thyroid cancer studies with at least one UK site that are recruiting, opening soon, or closed to recruitment in the last six months. It is built from the public trial registries ClinicalTrials.gov and ISRCTN, matched across registries by their cross-reference numbers. The WHO ICTRP imports its UK records from these two registries, and the EU CTIS register does not cover UK sites, so neither adds UK trials.

Status is taken from the UK sites listed on ClinicalTrials.gov where available, and otherwise from the ISRCTN recruitment dates. Tumour site, setting, UK region and the one-line summaries are added by the editor to help browsing; they are not registry fields. Studies across several tumour types are included only when head and neck or thyroid cancer makes up a large part of the study, such as a dedicated cohort or several of the study arms.

Registries are not always up to date, and some NIHR portfolio studies are not registered on either. Always check the registry record and the local research team before discussing a study with a patient.
